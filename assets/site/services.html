Photo management and storage {{- template "ask4access" $IMMICH_ACCESS }}

Immich is a polished photo and video management system. It is designed for uploading, storing, and sharing media with as bells and whistles like face recognition and geotagging.

There are three ways you can use Immich:

Web
Visit $IMMICH_SUBDOMAIN.■ and sign in.
iOS
Download the Immich app, then supply the server URL https://$IMMICH_URL. Log in.
Android
Download the Immich app (or F-Droid), then supply the server URL https://$IMMICH_URL. Log in.

When using the iOS or Android app, after logging in you can select albums on the device to back up (tap on the icon next to the top right corner). I recommend Camera on Android and Recent on iOS.

Migrating from Google Photos or iCloud

With a little effort, it's not too hard to migrate from backup drives or cloud storage, thanks to the tool immich-go. If you're happy running a simple command line tool, you can download the appropriate binary for your system, and figure out how to use it.

Document management {{- template "ask4access" $PAPERLESS_ACCESS }}

Paperless is a document management system, designed for scanning, storing, and tagging documents such as receipts, bills, letters, and manuals.

Because paperwork and filing is no fun, it learns from the labels and categories you assign to documents, and will automatically apply them to new documents. Simply throw everything in, and rely on the tagging and full-text search to dig out documents with ease later.

You can use Paperless a few different ways:

Web
Visit $PAPERLESS_SUBDOMAIN.■ and sign in.
iOS
You may download the 3rd party Swift Paperless. QuickScan also has built-in support for Paperless.
Android
There's Paperless Mobile (or F-Droid). It hasn't been updated in a while though.
